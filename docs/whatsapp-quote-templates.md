# WhatsApp Quote Templates

Copy, fill in the `[brackets]`, paste into WhatsApp.

---

## 1. Full Quote (new customer)

```
Hi [Name], thanks for your order! Here's your quote:

📋 ORDER SUMMARY
• Item: [e.g. 5,000 × A5 menus]
• Spec: [double-sided, full colour, 130gsm gloss]
• Finish: [folded / flat / etc.]
• Delivery: [address or collection]

💷 TOTAL: £[amount] (incl. VAT, delivery)

💳 PAYMENT
Secure payment link:
[paste Stripe link]

📌 NEXT STEPS
1. Once payment is received, we send your design proof within [24–48 hrs]
2. You approve the proof (or request changes)
3. We print and dispatch — delivery in [X working days]

Please note: design work and printing only begin after payment is confirmed.

Any questions, just reply here. Thanks!
— [Your name], Takeaway Menu Co.
```

---

## 2. Short Quote (repeat customer)

```
Hi [Name] 👋

Quote for your reorder:
• [Item + spec]
• Total: £[amount]

Pay here: [Stripe link]

Once paid, we'll start the design proof — same turnaround as last time.

Thanks!
```

---

## 3. Reorder (same as last time)

```
Hi [Name], confirming your reorder:

• Same as last order: [Item + spec]
• Total: £[amount]

Pay: [Stripe link]

Print starts as soon as payment lands. No new proof needed unless you want changes — just reply if so.

Cheers!
```

---

## 4. Tone variants

### More formal
> Replace the opener with: `Dear [Name], thank you for your enquiry. Please find your quote below:`
> Replace the closer with: `Kind regards, [Your name]`

### More casual
> Opener: `Hey [Name]! 🙌 Here's the quote for your order:`
> Closer: `Cheers — [Your name] 🚀`

---

## Payment-received follow-up

Once Stripe notifies you of payment, send:

```
Payment received ✅ Thanks [Name]!

Your order is now with our design team. We'll send your proof by [date/time].
```

---

## Tips

- **Stripe links:** create a Payment Link per quote in your Stripe dashboard (or use a generic one and confirm the amount in chat). Apple Pay / Google Pay are enabled by default.
- **Save customer details** after the first order so reorders are one message.
- **Always state "work starts after payment"** — protects you from no-shows and sets expectations.
