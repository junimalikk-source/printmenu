The short version

What is pizza takeaway menu printing?

Pizza takeaway menu printing is the design and print production of menus built specifically for UK pizza takeaways, pizzerias and fast food pizza shops. Unlike generic menu printing, cuisine-specific pizza menu printing is built around the size and topping grid, the deal-led layout that drives pizza orders, meal-deal bundles, build-your-own topping lists and the allergen labelling pizza requires (gluten, milk and soya in particular).

The standard specification across the UK trade is A4 double-sided, full colour CMYK, on 130gsm gloss paper, printed via offset litho for runs of 10,000 or more. Pizza takeaways reprint more often than most cuisines because deal and meal-deal pricing changes frequently, so many shops reprint every 3 to 4 months for door drop campaigns.

The anatomy of a pizza menu

The six sections every pizza takeaway menu needs.

The proven structure of a high-converting UK pizza takeaway menu, in the order customers read it.

1

Deals and meal deals

Meal deals, two-for-one, family bundles, student and collection offers. On a pizza menu the deals go first, not last. They are the single biggest driver of order value, so they earn the top of the front page with bold pricing and clear inclusions.

2

The pizza size and topping grid

Your signature and classic pizzas listed down the side, sizes across the top (9, 12, 14, 16 inch), prices in a clean grid. This is the largest and most important block on the menu and takes 40 to 50 percent of the space.

3

Build your own and extra toppings

A base price plus a clear topping list (pepperoni, mushroom, peppers, sweetcorn, jalapeños, chicken, ham). Customers who build their own spend more, so the topping list needs to be easy to read and priced consistently.

4

Sides, wings and loaded fries

Garlic bread, dough balls, chicken wings, loaded fries, onion rings, mozzarella sticks, salads. Sides lift the average order and sit naturally after the pizzas where customers add to their basket before checkout.

5

Drinks and desserts

Soft drinks, bottles, cans, milkshakes, plus desserts like chocolate cookie dough, churros, ice cream and Ben & Jerry's. A clear drinks and dessert section captures the impulse add-on that completes the meal-deal upsell.

6

Contact, delivery and allergens

Phone number, opening hours, delivery radius, minimum order, and online ordering platforms (Just Eat, Deliveroo, Uber Eats) at the foot. Allergen symbols and a gluten-free base note belong here too, since gluten is the key allergen for pizza.

Compliance, handled

Allergen and gluten-free labelling on pizza menus.

Pizza is a high-allergen product. Gluten, milk and soya appear on almost every pizza, and nuts, sesame and egg appear across sides and desserts. All of these are among the 14 allergens regulated under UK food information law. We label each dish with the correct symbols so your customers order safely and your business stays compliant.

Gluten-free bases are a growing request, so we make gluten-free options clear and easy to find on the menu. Send us your allergen data alongside your menu content and we integrate the symbols cleanly into the layout. Vegetarian (V) and vegan (VG) symbols are included at no extra charge.

Questions, answered

Pizza takeaway menu printing FAQs

Common questions from UK pizza takeaway and pizzeria owners.

How much does pizza takeaway menu printing cost in the UK?

A4 pizza takeaway menus cost £425 for 10,000 copies, printed full colour CMYK on 130gsm gloss both sides. A5 menus start at £200 for 10,000. Free design, free meal-deal layout, and free UK delivery are included on every order.

What size works best for a pizza takeaway menu?

A4 double-sided is the standard for UK pizza takeaway menus. It fits the pizza range, sizes and toppings grid, sides, drinks and deals without crowding. Pizza shops with large side and deal ranges use A4+ extended or A3. Single-page flyers and simple menus use A5.

Can you design a pizza size and topping grid?

Yes, the size and topping grid is the centre of a pizza menu and we lay it out for fast scanning. We show pizzas down the side, sizes across the top (9 inch, 12 inch, 14 inch, 16 inch), and prices in a clean grid. Build-your-own topping lists sit alongside.

How often should a pizza takeaway reprint its menu?

Most UK pizza takeaways reprint every 3 to 6 months for door drop campaigns and to refresh deal pricing. High-volume shops reprint quarterly. Deals and meal-deal pricing change more often than dishes, so pizza menus tend to reprint more frequently than other cuisines.

Do you provide pizza food photography for menus?

Yes, we hold a 20-year stock library of pizza photography covering margheritas, pepperoni, meat feasts, vegetarian and loaded pizzas, plus sides like garlic bread, wings, loaded fries and desserts. You can use our images free, supply your own, or book a custom photoshoot.

How long does pizza takeaway menu printing take?

3 to 5 working days from artwork approval to your door. The full cycle takes 5 to 10 working days from first contact: 1 to 2 days for design, 1 to 2 days for proof revisions, then 3 to 5 days for offset litho printing and free UK delivery.

Do you include allergen information on pizza menus?

Yes, we include allergen symbols for all 14 UK-regulated allergens. Pizza menus most often require gluten, milk and soya labelling, plus nut and sesame notes where relevant. UK food information regulations require accurate allergen disclosure on every printed menu.

Do I pay VAT on pizza takeaway menu printing?

No, printed pizza takeaway menus are zero-rated for VAT under HMRC's VAT Notice 701/10 on printed matter. The price you see is the price you pay, no VAT added. Always confirm with your accountant for your specific business setup.
