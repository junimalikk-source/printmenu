The six sections every Chinese takeaway menu needs.

The proven anatomy of a high-converting UK Chinese takeaway menu.

1

Starters and appetisers

Spring rolls, prawn crackers, crispy duck, sesame prawn toast, spare ribs, salt and pepper chicken wings. The top-left of the menu front. Prawn crackers work well as the cheapest entry point and an anchor for the order total.

2

Soups

Wonton, hot and sour, sweetcorn and chicken, mushroom. Soups are typically a short dedicated section between starters and mains. UK Chinese takeaway customers expect soup to sit clearly between starters and the main course, never buried in the sides section.

3

Mains grouped by protein

Chicken, beef, pork, prawns, vegetables, tofu. Within each protein group, list dishes from mild to bold (sweet and sour, black bean, oyster sauce, kung po, Szechuan). This is the largest section and takes 40 to 60 percent of menu space.

4

Noodles, fried rice and chow mein

Egg fried rice, special fried rice, boiled rice, Singapore noodles, chow mein, lo mein, crispy noodles. Position this section after mains where customers naturally decide on their base. Portion notes can sit here where they help customers choose.

5

Dim sum and chef's specials

Har gow, siu mai, char siu bao, cheung fun, turnip cake. Chef's specials and Peking duck orders sit here. A tinted background or border helps these higher-margin dishes stand out. Dim sum availability (lunch only, weekends only) must be clearly noted.

6

Desserts, drinks and contact

Toffee banana, toffee apple, ice cream, lychees, soft drinks, Chinese tea. Phone number, opening hours, delivery radius and online ordering platforms (Just Eat, Deliveroo, Uber Eats) sit at the foot. Allergen symbols belong here too.

Allergen labelling on Chinese takeaway menus.

UK Chinese takeaway customers increasingly expect clear allergen information. Chinese cuisine regularly uses peanuts, tree nuts, shellfish, soy, sesame, gluten and celery, all of which are among the 14 allergens regulated under UK food information law. We label menus with the correct symbols for each dish so your customers can order safely and your business stays compliant.

Send us your allergen data alongside your menu content and we integrate the symbols cleanly into the layout without cluttering the visual design. Vegetarian (V) and vegan (VG) symbols are included at no extra charge.

Chinese takeaway menu printing FAQs

Common questions from UK Chinese takeaway and restaurant owners.

How much does Chinese takeaway menu printing cost in the UK?

A4 Chinese takeaway menus cost £425 for 10,000 copies, printed full colour CMYK on 130gsm gloss both sides. A5 menus start at £200 for 10,000. Free design, free Chinese script typesetting, and free UK delivery are included on every order.

Can you typeset Chinese takeaway menus in Simplified or Traditional Chinese?

Yes, we typeset Chinese takeaway menus in both Simplified Chinese (used in mainland China) and Traditional Chinese (used in Hong Kong and Taiwan) every week. Send your dish names in your preferred script and we handle the layout, font selection, proofing and print-ready PDF. No extra charge.

What size works best for Chinese takeaway menus?

A4 double-sided is the standard for UK Chinese takeaway menus. It fits 60 to 100 dishes across starters, soups, mains by protein, noodles, rice and sides without crowding. Large Chinese restaurants with extensive dim sum menus use A4+ extended or A3. Compact menus under 40 dishes use A5.

How often should a Chinese takeaway reprint its menu?

Most UK Chinese takeaways reprint every 4 to 6 months for door drop campaigns. High-volume restaurants in London, Manchester and Birmingham reprint quarterly. Plan reprints ahead of Chinese New Year, allowing 2 to 3 weeks lead time as demand spikes in January and February.

How long does Chinese takeaway menu printing take?

3 to 5 working days from artwork approval to your door. The full cycle takes 5 to 10 working days from first contact: 1 to 2 days for design, 1 to 2 days for proof revisions, then 3 to 5 days for offset litho printing and free UK delivery.

Do you include allergen information on Chinese takeaway menus?

Yes, we include allergen symbols for all 14 UK-regulated allergens including gluten, nuts, shellfish, soy, sesame and celery. Chinese takeaway menus often require peanut, shellfish and gluten labelling. UK food information regulations require accurate allergen disclosure on every printed menu.

Do I pay VAT on Chinese takeaway menu printing?

No, printed Chinese takeaway menus are zero-rated for VAT under HMRC's VAT Notice 701/10 on printed matter. The price you see is the price you pay, no VAT added. Always confirm with your accountant for your specific business setup.
