Indian takeaway menu printing: FAQs

Common questions from Indian takeaway owners across the UK.

How much does Indian takeaway menu printing cost?

A4 Indian takeaway menus start at £425 for 10,000 copies, printed full colour on 130gsm gloss both sides. A5 starts at £200 for 10,000. Free design and free UK delivery are included with every order. See the pricing table above for the full breakdown.

Can you typeset Indian menus in Bengali or Urdu?

Yes. We typeset Indian takeaway menus in English, Bengali, Urdu and Hindi every week. Send your dish names in your preferred script and we handle the layout, proofing and print-ready PDF. No extra charge for non-English scripts.

What is the best size for an Indian takeaway menu?

A4 double-sided is the most popular size for Indian takeaway menus. It gives you enough room for starters, mains by meat type, biryani, breads, sides and desserts without crowding. Larger menus with over 100 dishes often use A4+ or A3. Call us with your dish count for a recommendation.

Do you provide food photography for Indian menus?

Yes. We hold a stock library of Indian food photography covering curries, biryanis, tandoori dishes, breads and starters. You can use our images free of charge, or supply your own. Custom food photoshoots are available on request.

How long does Indian takeaway menu printing take?

3 to 5 working days after artwork approval. We send a PDF proof before printing. Once you approve, your menus are printed, packed and dispatched with free UK delivery to your door.

What paper stock do you use for Indian takeaway menus?

Standard is 130gsm gloss, full colour both sides. This is the most popular stock for Indian curry house menus — it reproduces photography well and is durable enough to handle daily use. Heavier stocks and silk finishes are available on request.

Ready to print your Indian takeaway menus?

Call or WhatsApp us with your dish count, preferred size and delivery postcode. We will quote you in minutes and send a design proof within 24 hours.
