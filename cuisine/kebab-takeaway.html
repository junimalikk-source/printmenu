The short version

What is kebab takeaway menu printing?

Kebab takeaway menu printing is menu design and print built specifically for UK kebab shops and grill takeaways. Unlike a generic menu template, a kebab menu has to handle a long, multi-section list: doner, shish and mixed grills, plus the pizzas, burgers, wraps, fried chicken and sides that most UK kebab shops sell alongside. It puts your deal boxes where they earn the most, marks halal status clearly, and carries the full allergen disclosure that grilled and fried food needs.

The layout does the selling. The print makes it last. Menus are produced A4 double-sided, full colour, on 130gsm gloss for runs of 10,000 or more. Kebab shops often carry 80 or more items across four or five sections, so many choose A4+ extended or A3 to fit the full range without crowding, and reprint every 3 to 6 months to keep deal prices current.

The anatomy of a kebab menu

The six sections every kebab takeaway menu needs.

The proven structure of a high-converting UK kebab shop menu, in the order your customers read it.

1

Deals and meal deals

Meal deals, mixed grill for two, family feasts, student offers and late-night bundles. On a kebab menu the deals earn the top of the front page. They lift the average order more than anything else on the menu, so give them bold pricing, clear inclusions and a boxed layout that jumps out from the main list.

2

Kebabs: doner, shish and mixed grills

Doner, chicken and lamb shish, kofte, wings, mixed grill, shish wraps. This is what customers come for, so it earns pride of place. List by meat and by serving style (in pitta, in naan, as a platter, as a wrap) with clear sizes where you offer them, so nobody has to ask.

3

Pizzas, burgers and fried chicken

Most UK kebab shops sell pizzas, burgers and fried chicken next to the grill. Give each its own clear block so the menu does not blur into one endless list. Pizza sizes and toppings, burger patty and topping options, and chicken by pieces or bucket all need their own consistent pricing.

4

Wraps, sides and loaded fries

Wraps, chips, cheesy chips, loaded fries, onion rings, garlic bread, salads, dips and sauces. Sides lift the average order and sit naturally after the mains, where customers add to the basket before they check out. A clear dips and sauces list nudges the small add-ons that add up fast.

5

Drinks and desserts

Soft drinks, cans, bottles, milkshakes, plus baklava, ice cream and cookie dough. A clean drinks and dessert section catches the impulse add-on that completes a meal deal and rounds out a late-night order, turning a single kebab into a full basket.

6

Contact, delivery and allergens

Phone number, opening hours (late-night hours included), delivery radius, minimum order, and your ordering platforms (Just Eat, Deliveroo, Uber Eats) at the foot. Halal certification and allergen symbols belong here too, with gluten and sesame marked clearly for grilled and fried food.

Trust and compliance

Halal and allergen labelling that customers trust.

UK kebab shop customers check for halal before they order, so we display your certification prominently on the front of the menu, from your local authority or your supplier's scheme, where Muslim customers look for it first. That clarity builds trust and wins repeat orders. Grilled and fried food commonly involves gluten, sesame, milk, egg, mustard and soya, all among the 14 allergens regulated under UK food information law.

Send us your halal logo and allergen data with your menu content, and we work the symbols cleanly into the design without cluttering it. Vegetarian (V) and vegan (VG) symbols are included at no extra charge, which matters more each year as kebab shops add falafel and halloumi.

Questions, answered

Kebab takeaway menu printing FAQs

Common questions from UK kebab shop and grill takeaway owners.

How much does kebab takeaway menu printing cost in the UK?

A4 kebab takeaway menus cost £425 for 10,000 copies, printed full colour on 130gsm gloss both sides. A5 menus start at £200 for 10,000. Free design, free deal layout, and free UK delivery are included on every order, so the price you see is the price you pay.

What size works best for a kebab shop menu?

Kebab shops carry long menus, so A4+ extended or A3 often works best to fit doner, grills, pizzas, burgers, chicken, sides and deals without crowding. Standard A4 double-sided suits most shops. A5 suits compact menus or single-page flyers under 40 items.

Can you print halal certification on kebab menus?

Yes, we display halal certification logos clearly on kebab takeaway menus, from your local authority or supplier scheme. Send us your halal logo and we position it prominently on the front, where customers look before they order. Most UK kebab shops mark halal status clearly, and it wins trust.

How often should a kebab shop reprint its menu?

Most UK kebab shops reprint every 3 to 6 months for door drop campaigns and to refresh deal pricing. Busy shops reprint quarterly. Deals and meal-deal prices change more often than the grill menu, so kebab menus tend to reprint more frequently than curry house menus.

Do you provide food photography for kebab menus?

Yes, we hold a 20-year stock library covering doner, shish, mixed grills, burgers, loaded fries, wraps and pizzas. You can use our images free, supply your own, or book a custom photoshoot. We match the warm, hungry tones that sell grilled food from the page.

How long does kebab takeaway menu printing take?

3 to 5 working days from artwork approval to your door. The full cycle takes 5 to 10 working days from first contact: 1 to 2 days for design, 1 to 2 days for proof revisions, then 3 to 5 days for printing and free UK delivery.

Do I pay VAT on kebab takeaway menu printing?

No, printed kebab takeaway menus are zero-rated for VAT under HMRC's VAT Notice 701/10 on printed matter. The price you see is the price you pay, no VAT added. Always confirm with your accountant for your specific business setup, but the zero-rating is standard for menus.
