Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-15

PrintMenu.co.uk (we, us, our) is based in Bradford, United Kingdom. This page explains what personal data we collect when you use this website.

What we collect

When you submit our quote form we collect: your name, phone number, optional email, the size and quantity of menus you're interested in, and any notes you add. We use this only to send you a quote and follow up.

How long we keep it

We retain quote enquiries for up to 24 months. You can ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing hello@printmenu.co.uk.

Cookies

This website does not set tracking cookies.
