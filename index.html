Takeaway menu printing — frequently asked questions

Everything customers ask before placing their first order.

What paper weight do you print takeaway menus on?

All our takeaway menus are printed on 130gsm paper, full colour, both sides. It's the standard weight British takeaways have used for years — premium feel, posts well, and works for door-drops or in-store.

How long does takeaway menu printing take?

3–5 working days from artwork approval to your door. We start production the day you sign off the design proof.

Do you design the menu for me?

Yes — menu design is included free with every order. Send us your dishes, prices, logo and any branding, and we lay out a print-ready design for you to approve before we print.

Do you deliver takeaway menus across the UK?

Yes — free UK delivery is included on every order, with no minimum. We post menus to takeaways anywhere in England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.

What sizes of takeaway menu can you print?

A5, A4, A4+ extended, and A3. A4 is by far the most popular for takeaway menus. A5 suits compact menus, A3 is used for wall menus and large fold-outs.

What is the minimum order for menu printing?

Our smallest run is 10,000 menus. Lower quantities aren't cost-effective for offset printing — and at 10,000 our prices already start at £200 (A5) or £425 (A4).

Can I see a proof before you print?

Yes. We send a digital proof for you to approve before anything goes to press. Revisions are free until you're happy.

How much does it cost to print 10,000 A4 takeaway menus?

£425 including free design and free UK delivery. With the current May Sale (ends 31 May) that's a 20% saving on the standard rate.
