Why cuisine-specific menu design matters.

A generic menu template costs you orders. A cuisine-built menu earns them back.

📊

Customers scan menus in cuisine patterns

An Indian takeaway customer looks for starters, then mains by meat type, then biryani, then breads. A pizza customer looks for deals first, then sizes, then toppings. We lay your menu out in the order your customers expect, so they find dishes faster and order more.

🎨

Photography style sets the cuisine tone

Indian curry photography needs warm, rich tones. Pizza needs hot, oven-fresh shots. Sushi needs clean, precise styling. Our stock library covers every major UK cuisine, built up over 20 years of menu work. You can use our imagery free, or send your own.

💷

Price-point bands differ by cuisine

An £8 main looks premium on a fish and chip menu and budget on a Thai menu. We typeset prices with the visual weight each cuisine expects, including how to display set meals, family deals, meal-for-one offers and bundle discounts that drive average order value.

Cuisine menu printing: frequently asked questions

Everything UK takeaway owners ask about cuisine-specific design.

Do you specialise in any particular cuisine?

We print takeaway menus for every UK cuisine. Indian and Chinese are our highest-volume categories, followed by pizza, kebab and halal. We have laid out menus in over a dozen cuisines and handle scripts including Bengali, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic, Chinese and Turkish.

Why does cuisine matter when designing a takeaway menu?

Each cuisine has its own dish ordering, layout norms and customer expectations. An Indian menu reads differently from a pizza menu. Cuisine-specific design improves scan speed, increases average order value, and builds trust with customers familiar with the category.

Can you typeset menus in non-English scripts?

Yes, we typeset takeaway menus in Bengali, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic, Chinese and Turkish scripts every week. Send your dish names in your preferred language. We handle the layout, the proofing and the print-ready PDF with all scripts rendered correctly.

What sizes work best for each cuisine?

A4 suits most cuisines including Indian, Chinese, pizza and kebab. A5 works for fish and chips and compact menus under 40 dishes. A3 fits long Chinese menus, halal grill houses with mixed sections, and wall menus. Call us with your dish count for a recommendation.

Do you provide food photography for cuisine-specific menus?

Yes, we hold a stock library of food imagery built over 20 years, covering Indian, Chinese, pizza, kebab, halal and pan-Asian dishes. You can use our images at no extra cost, or send your own photography. Custom food photoshoots are available on request.

How much does cuisine-specific menu design cost?

Nothing extra. Cuisine-specific menu design is included free with every print order. We never upsell design as an add-on. Send your dish list, logo and any imagery, and our in-house designers lay out a print-ready menu tailored to your cuisine.

Can I see examples of menus you have printed for my cuisine?

Yes, call or WhatsApp us with your cuisine type and we will send sample PDFs of recent menus we have printed in that category. We respect client confidentiality, so samples may be anonymised, but the layout and design approach will give you a clear reference.

What is the minimum order for cuisine-specific menus?

Our minimum is 10,000 menus across all cuisines because that is where offset litho printing becomes properly cheap. A5 menus start at £200 for 10,000, A4 menus from £425. Free design and free UK delivery are included regardless of cuisine.

Not sure which cuisine page to read?

Call or WhatsApp us with your cuisine type, dish count and delivery area. We will tell you the best size, quantity and layout for your menu and quote you in minutes.
